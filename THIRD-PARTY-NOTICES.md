## Material Symbols

The plugin icon, `src/Community.PowerToys.Run.Plugin.Claude/Images/newchat.light.png` and
`newchat.dark.png`, is rendered from the `asterisk` glyph of Google's Material Symbols.
The glyph is unmodified in shape; it is rasterised to 48 pixels and filled with a single
colour so that it stays readable on both PowerToys themes.
